Clause 1: This agreement is subject to a force majeure provision releasing both parties from obligations made impossible by extraordinary events.
Clause 2: This agreement shall terminate automatically upon expiration of the initial term unless renewed by mutual written consent.
Clause 3: If any provision of this agreement is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.
Clause 4: Confidential information exchanged between the parties shall be protected using at least the same degree of care as the receiving party uses for its own confidential information.
Clause 5: Each party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other from any claims, damages, or costs arising from breach of this agreement.
Clause 6: In no event shall either party be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages regardless of the cause.
Clause 7: Any modifications to this agreement must be made in writing and signed by authorised representatives of both parties.
